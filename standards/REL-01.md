# REL-01 Disaster recovery for tier-1 systems
Tier-1 (client-facing) systems must have disaster recovery in a second region,
with a recovery point objective of 15 minutes or less and a recovery time objective of 4 hours or less.
Single-region or single-instance deployments are not acceptable for tier-1 systems.
Backups kept only in the same region do not count as disaster recovery.
